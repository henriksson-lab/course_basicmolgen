# Course order

Students move top to bottom through the sidebar. The order follows the course book (Hartwell, *Genetics: From Genes to Genomes*, 5th ed. chapter numbers). The order lives in `_quarto.yml`. Folder names never change. Every unit has `lec.qmd`, `quiz.qmd`, `exercise.qmd` and `exercise-r.qmd`; ethics has no R page. The demo anchors are `#explore`, or `#explore-<x>` for a second demo.

## Part 1: Mendelian genetics (Ch 1–3)
1. intro-genetics (Ch 1)
2. mendel (Ch 2): demo `punnett`
3. probability-chisq (Ch 2): demo `chisq`
4. mendel-extensions (Ch 3): demo `epistasis`
5. pedigrees (Ch 2–3): demo `pedigree`

## Part 2: Chromosomes and mapping (Ch 4–5)
1. chromosome-theory (Ch 4): demo `meiosis`
2. linkage (Ch 5): demo `crossover`
3. three-point-cross (Ch 5): demo `threepoint`
4. lod-scores (Ch 5): demo `lod`

## Part 3: DNA, mutation and gene function (Ch 6–8)
1. dna-recap (Ch 6), a recap of 5MO103
2. mutation (Ch 7): demo `complementation`
3. gene-expression-recap (Ch 8), a recap of 5MO103

## Part 4: Genome analysis (Ch 9–11)
1. digital-dna (Ch 9): demos `gel` (#explore) and `pcr` (#explore-pcr)
2. genome-annotation (Ch 10): demo `orf`
3. genomic-variation (Ch 11)

## Part 5: Chromosomes in detail (Ch 12–15)
1. eukaryotic-chromosome (Ch 12)
2. rearrangements-aneuploidy (Ch 13): demo `meiosis` with `data-mode="nondisjunction"`, the demo owned by Part 2
3. bacterial-genetics-recap (Ch 14), a recap of 5MO103
4. organelle-inheritance (Ch 15): demo `bottleneck`

## Part 6: Gene regulation and development (Ch 16–19)
1. prokaryotic-regulation-recap (Ch 16): demo `lacoperon`
2. eukaryotic-regulation (Ch 17): demo `xinactivation`
3. rna-regulation (Ch 18)
4. development-model-organisms (Ch 19)

## Part 7: Cancer, populations and complex traits (Ch 20–22)
1. cancer-genetics (Ch 20): demo `twohit`
2. hardy-weinberg (Ch 21): demo `hwe`
3. drift-selection (Ch 21): demo `drift`
4. complex-traits (Ch 22): demos `heritability` (#explore) and `manhattan` (#explore-manhattan)

## Ethics (1 ECTS module)
1. ethics: lecture, quiz, and an exercise with cases and a literature search. There is no R page.
